October is when empty arenas start filling up again: two weeks of tune-up games between training camp and opening night.

The NBA preseason is the short stretch of exhibition games between training camp and opening night. Teams use it to test rotations, give rookies and fringe players real minutes and get their stars back into game shape, which is why the results matter far less than what coaches learn from them. Below you'll find how every team finished, the full 2026 calendar, and how the preseason works for anyone planning ahead for next October.

What the preseason records show (and don't)

The Celtics were the only team to get through October without a loss, and the Atlanta Hawks (4-1) and Golden State Warriors (4-2) were the only other teams to reach four wins. At the other end, the Kings went 0-5, while the defending champion New York Knicks and the Oklahoma City Thunder both finished 1-4.

That last detail is the best reminder of how little these exhibition records predict. Contenders routinely limit their stars to short stints or sit them entirely, while teams with open roster spots play their deepest benches for long stretches. A 4-0 or 0-5 October tells you more about who was on the floor than about who will be good in April.

2026 NBA Preseason Global and Neutral-Site Games

The league uses its exhibition schedule to take games to fans who rarely see the league in person. In 2026 that meant two international series and a pair of neutral-site games in the United States:

  • NBA Canada Games: the Raptors opened the exhibition slate against the Heat at Videotron Centre in Quebec City on October 3, then faced the Clippers at Rogers Arena in Vancouver on October 10.
  • NBA China Games: the Dallas Mavericks and Houston Rockets played twice at The Venetian Arena in Macao, on October 9 and October 11. Both teams opened camp a week early to make the trip.
  • Honolulu: the Golden State Warriors and LA Clippers met in Hawaii on October 4.
  • Boulder, Colorado: the Utah Jazz and Denver Nuggets played a college-town game on October 4.

When Does the NBA Preseason Start?

The NBA preseason starts in early October, a few days after training camps open at the end of September. It typically lasts about two weeks and wraps up a few days before opening night, leaving teams time to set their final rosters.

In 2026 that meant tune-up games from October 3 to October 16, with media day on September 28, camps opening September 29 and the regular season starting October 20. The 2027 preseason should follow the same early-October pattern, with exact dates confirmed when the NBA releases its schedule, which usually happens in August. How Does the NBA Preseason Work?

It's a series of exhibition games played after training camp opens. Teams usually schedule a handful of them, typically four to six. Going by the final standings above, 2026 teams played anywhere from three games (Cleveland, the Clippers and Houston) to six (Golden State and Minnesota).

Those games serve a few clear purposes:

  • Roster decisions: camp invitees, two-way candidates and veterans fighting for the last spots get real game minutes in front of the coaching staff before rosters are due the day before opening night.
  • Rotations and new systems: new coaches install their schemes, and teams with offseason additions see how lineups fit together against real opponents.
  • Conditioning and load management: established stars ramp up gradually. Many play limited minutes or skip some games entirely, especially veterans and players coming back from injuries.
  • Taking the game to new markets: international and neutral-site games, like the 2026 trips to Macao, Quebec City, Vancouver and Honolulu, help the league reach fans outside NBA cities.

Do NBA Preseason Games Count?

No. These games are exhibitions. Wins and losses don't count toward the regular-season standings, and individual stats don't count toward season or career totals, statistical titles or awards. That's why a team can go 0-5 in October and still be a contender: the slate is wiped clean on opening night.

The NBA Cup is a different case. Its group-stage games are part of the regular season and do count in the standings, but the Cup only starts after opening night and has nothing to do with these October exhibitions.

How to Watch NBA Preseason Games

Most preseason games aren't on national TV, so where you watch depends on the game and where you live. In 2026, the games aired on:

  • NBA TV: the main national home of these games, including the opener in Quebec City.
  • ESPN and ESPN2: selected national games.
  • NBC and Peacock: selected national games, part of NBC's first seasons back with the NBA.
  • NBA League Pass: out-of-market preseason games.
  • Regional sports networks and local channels, which carry many teams' exhibition games in their home markets.

To stream without cable, live TV services such as YouTube TV carry NBA TV, ESPN and NBC, and Peacock streams the NBC games. For the regular season, see our full guide on how to watch NBA games live. If you just want the best plays, the NBA posts preseason highlights on its official app and YouTube channel shortly after each game.
