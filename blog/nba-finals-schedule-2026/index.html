2026 NBA Finals Schedule: Every Game, Every Score

The Knicks beat the Spurs 4-1 to capture their first title since 1973. Here's the full game-by-game schedule and results from the 2026 NBA Finals.

By NBA GRID Staff • June 1, 2026 • 4 min read

The 2026 NBA Finals pitted the East's No. 1 seed New York Knicks against the West's surprise finalist, the San Antonio Spurs. New York closed out the series in five games, ending a 53-year championship drought. Below is the full schedule and results.

Game 1 tipped off June 3 on ABC at Madison Square Garden.

KEY TAKEAWAY

The Knicks won the series 4-1, closing it out with a 94-90 Game 5 victory to claim their first championship since 1973.

How the Series Turned

New York stole home-court advantage with a tight Game 2 win, then survived a Spurs rally in San Antonio to take a commanding 3-1 lead before closing it out at home. The Knicks' size and defensive discipline neutralized San Antonio's pace-and-space attack for most of the series.
