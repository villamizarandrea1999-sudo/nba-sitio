2026 NBA Free Agency Tracker: Every Major Move

LeBron heads to Philly, Giannis lands in Miami, and the league's contender map gets redrawn. Here's every major signing from the 2026 offseason.

By NBA GRID Staff • July 10, 2026 • 5 min read

The 2026 NBA free agency period opened June 30, with official signings beginning July 6. It turned into one of the most consequential offseasons in years, headlined by two future Hall of Famers changing teams. Here's the full tracker.

Several contenders reshaped their rosters within days of the negotiating window opening.

KEY TAKEAWAY

LeBron James signed with the Philadelphia 76ers, who also traded for Jaylen Brown, while Giannis Antetokounmpo was traded to the Miami Heat to team up with Bam Adebayo.

The Biggest Moves

  • LeBron James signs with Philadelphia — the 76ers also traded for Jaylen Brown the same summer, instantly becoming a top-three roster.
  • Giannis Antetokounmpo traded to Miami — pairing him with Bam Adebayo and the Heat's infrastructure. Full story: Giannis Traded to Miami.
  • Bradley Beal returns to the L.A. Clippers — read more in Bradley Beal Returns to LA.
  • Donovan Mitchell signs a $273M extension with Cleveland — details in Donovan Mitchell's extension.
  • Dillon Brooks re-signs with Phoenix on a multi-year extension.
