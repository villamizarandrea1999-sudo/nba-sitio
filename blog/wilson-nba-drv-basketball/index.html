Wilson NBA DRV Basketball: Specs & Price

A quick, honest breakdown of Wilson's recreational NBA-licensed basketball line — before you decide it's the right ball for your court.

By Staff Reporter • 3 min read

The Wilson NBA DRV basketball is part of Wilson's officially licensed NBA recreational lineup — built for everyday pickup games and practice rather than tournament play. It's a common search for anyone comparing entry-level NBA-branded basketballs before buying.

The DRV's composite cover is built for exactly this — repeated outdoor contact without breaking down.

QUICK TAKEAWAY

If you play mostly outdoors, composite/rubber covers like the DRV line hold up far better than leather over time.

Where It Fits in Wilson's NBA Lineup

TIER COVER MATERIAL BEST FOR

Why Buy It

  • Officially licensed — carries real NBA branding without the game-ball price tag.
  • Composite/rubber durability — handles outdoor asphalt and mixed-use courts better than leather.
  • Budget-friendly — priced well below the official leather game ball, typically in the mid-range recreational bracket.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Wilson NBA DRV the same ball used in NBA games?

No — the in-game ball is the leather NBA Official Game Ball. The DRV line is Wilson's composite/rubber recreational tier.

Is the Wilson NBA DRV good for outdoor courts?

Yes, composite/rubber covers like the DRV's generally hold up better outdoors than premium leather balls.

Who should buy the Wilson NBA DRV basketball?

Casual players and anyone on outdoor or mixed-surface courts who wants an officially licensed NBA ball at a lower price point.
