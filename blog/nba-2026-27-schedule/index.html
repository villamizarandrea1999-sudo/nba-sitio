NBA Releases 2026-27 Schedule: Opening Night, Christmas Games & Key Dates

The season tips off October 20 and wraps up April 11, 2027, with a Knicks-Spurs Finals rematch anchoring a stacked Christmas Day slate.

By NBA GRID Staff • August 15, 2026 • 4 min read

The NBA has released its full schedule for the 2026-27 season. The regular season begins Tuesday, October 20, 2026, and runs through April 11, 2027, setting the stage for another marquee campaign that includes international games, a loaded Christmas Day lineup and a headline-grabbing opening night.

Opening night closes with a rematch of the 2026 Western Conference Finals between the Thunder and Spurs.

KEY TAKEAWAY

The Knicks will raise their 2025-26 championship banner on opening night before hosting the 76ers, then face the Spurs again on Christmas Day in a rematch of last season's Finals.

Opening Night: October 20

The season opens with an NBC tripleheader: Boston Celtics at Detroit Pistons (3 p.m. ET), Philadelphia 76ers at New York Knicks (7 p.m. ET) — where the Knicks raise their championship banner — and Oklahoma City Thunder at San Antonio Spurs (9:30 p.m. ET), a rematch of the 2026 Western Conference Finals.

Christmas Day: A Five-Game Showcase

The NBA's Christmas Day slate features five marquee matchups: San Antonio Spurs vs. New York Knicks, a direct rematch of the 2026 NBA Finals; Miami Heat vs. Boston Celtics; Philadelphia 76ers vs. Los Angeles Lakers; Oklahoma City Thunder vs. Minnesota Timberwolves; and Denver Nuggets vs. Golden State Warriors, all airing on ABC/ESPN.

A Full Slate of Storylines

Beyond the headline dates, the league confirmed its broadcast partners for the season — Disney (ABC/ESPN), NBCUniversal (NBC/Peacock) and Amazon Prime Video — along with a full international schedule. The calendar sets up a season-long narrative arc from opening night through the April 11 regular-season finale.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the 2026-27 NBA season start?

The 2026-27 NBA regular season begins Tuesday, October 20, 2026, with a tripleheader on NBC: Boston at Detroit, Philadelphia at New York, and Oklahoma City at San Antonio.

When does the 2026-27 NBA season end?

The 2026-27 regular season is scheduled to conclude on April 11, 2027, ahead of the playoffs.

What games are on Christmas Day 2026?

The NBA's Christmas Day slate features five games, headlined by a rematch of the 2026 NBA Finals between the New York Knicks and San Antonio Spurs, alongside Miami-Boston, Philadelphia-Lakers, Oklahoma City-Minnesota and Denver-Golden State.

What is the marquee opening night matchup?

Opening night closes with a rematch of the 2026 Western Conference Finals between the Oklahoma City Thunder and San Antonio Spurs, while the Knicks raise their 2025-26 championship banner earlier that night against the 76ers.
