The rivalry really started in Las Vegas on December 16, 2025, when the Knicks beat the Spurs 124-113 to win the NBA Cup and Brunson was named tournament MVP. San Antonio got one back on New Year's Eve, 134-132. Then the Knicks won by 25 at the Garden in March, a preview nobody took seriously until June.

How the Knicks Beat the Spurs in the 2026 NBA Finals

The Spurs had home-court advantage as the No. 2 seed, and the Knicks took it away right away. In Game 1, New York erased a 14-point second-half deficit, closed on an 11-0 run and won 105-95. According to the AP, that made the Knicks the first team ever to beat San Antonio in Game 1 of a Finals (the Spurs had been 6-0). Two nights later they won again, 105-104, stretching their playoff winning streak to 13. The Spurs finally ended that streak at the Garden in Game 3, 115-111.

Game 4 was the one that broke them. The Spurs led by as many as 29 points, and the Knicks still came back to win 107-106 for a 3-1 series lead, which ESPN called a record rally. Back in San Antonio for Game 5, Jalen Brunson dropped 45 points in a 94-90 win that clinched New York's first championship since 1973.

Brunson was the obvious Finals MVP at 32.6 points per game. OG Anunoby was the quiet difference-maker: 21.2 points a game on 50% from three. Wembanyama averaged 26.0 points, 11.2 rebounds and 3.6 blocks, but the Knicks made him work for everything. It always comes back to Wembanyama. He's averaging over 30 points and over 4 blocks a game, and he can wreck a game on either end. In June he shot just 27% from three (9 of 33), and New York will happily give him those looks again.

Brunson is the guy San Antonio has to solve. He scored 30, 36 and 45 in three of the Knicks' four Finals wins. Expect the Spurs to throw length at him and force him to beat them as a passer. Towns is the swing piece: if he's pulling Wembanyama out to the three-point line, the paint opens up for Brunson. And don't sleep on Dylan Harper. San Antonio's second-year guard averaged 18 points a game off the bench in the Finals.

Spurs vs Knicks Prediction

This is an early lean, not a pick against the spread. We'll update it once injury reports and lines are out the week of the game.

Early lean: Spurs. The Knicks have owned this matchup, and Christmas at the Garden is about as tough a road game as there is. But this San Antonio team is playing better than the one that lost in June. The Spurs carry a 10-game win streak and a Wembanyama who's playing at an MVP level, and ESPN's matchup predictor agrees, giving San Antonio a 53.9% chance to win as of November 11. New York's best path is the Finals formula: slow the game down, keep Wembanyama off the glass, and let Brunson take over late in close games. If it's within five points with three minutes left, flip this lean.
