NBA Mascots: The Full List by Team

From Benny the Bull to Rocky the Mountain Lion — every official NBA mascot, and the handful of teams that skip the costume entirely.

By Staff Reporter • 4 min read

Most NBA arenas have a second star running the sideline every night: the mascot. Some have been part of their franchise's identity for over 50 years; a few teams skip the costume altogether. Here's the full team-by-team rundown.

Mascots are part of the in-arena show at nearly every NBA venue.

The Teams Without a Mascot

Not every franchise plays the mascot game. The Los Angeles Lakers, New York Knicks, and Brooklyn Nets are the most notable holdouts — historically leaning on courtside celebrity culture, dance squads, and in-arena hype instead of a costumed character.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which NBA teams don't have a mascot?

The Lakers, Knicks, and Nets are the most notable NBA franchises without an official on-court mascot.

What is the most famous NBA mascot?

Benny the Bull of the Chicago Bulls, performing since the 1969-70 season, is one of the most recognizable mascots in the league.

Why don't the Lakers have a mascot?

The Lakers have historically relied on their star-studded courtside crowd and cheerleading squad for in-arena entertainment instead.
