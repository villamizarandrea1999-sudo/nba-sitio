Anthony Davis and the Wizards Table Extension Talks Until the Regular Season

The big man, acquired in a trade with Dallas at the February deadline, is now extension-eligible for $275 million — but both sides would rather wait.

By NBA GRID Staff • August 6, 2026 • 4 min read

Thursday, August 6, marked the first day Anthony Davis was eligible to sign a contract extension with the Washington Wizards. However, according to CBS Sports, both sides have decided to table any "meaningful" conversations until the 2026-27 regular season is underway.

Davis has yet to play a single game for the Wizards since arriving via trade.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Davis has yet to play a single official game for the Wizards: he arrived in the February 2026 trade deadline deal, but the season ended before he could debut.

Why Wait

A source cited by CBS Sports described the decision as a way to let the first 20 games or so of the season "marinate," giving both Davis and the franchise time to assess whether there's a solid foundation for a long-term extension.

The Current Contract's Numbers

The 2026-27 season is the final fully guaranteed year of Davis' current contract, carrying a $58.5 million cap hit, followed by a $62.8 million player option for 2027-28. If an extension is reached, Davis would be eligible for a four-year, $275 million deal, according to the same reports.

The Health and Fit Factor

Washington is in the middle of a rebuild, and Davis' arrival represents a mid-term bet. Davis has been one of the league's most dominant players when healthy, but also one of the most affected by recurring physical issues throughout his career. For a rebuilding franchise, evaluating his real availability before committing $275 million over four years is a risk-management decision.

What's Next on the Calendar

The next relevant milestone will be Davis' actual debut with the Wizards, which didn't happen in 2025-26 because the trade closed after the deadline. How he adapts physically and competitively over the first weeks of the season will, per the reports, determine whether talks move forward.
