NBA Highlights: Where to Watch Daily

Missed last night's game? Here's where NBA highlights actually post first, and how to skip the copyright-stricken re-uploads.

By Staff Reporter • 4 min read

If you're chasing NBA highlights the morning after a big game, timing and source matter more than most fans realize. Official channels post faster and stay up longer than random re-uploads, which get pulled almost as fast as they're posted.

Best Sources for NBA Highlights

  • NBA App & NBA.com — condensed games and top-10 play compilations, usually live within one to two hours of the final buzzer.
  • NBA on YouTube — the league's official channel posts full-game recaps and highlight reels the same night.
  • ESPN & Bleacher Report — fast turnaround on nationally relevant games and viral individual plays.
  • Team-specific social accounts — often the quickest source for buzzer-beaters and highlight-reel dunks, sometimes within minutes.

Why Some Highlight Clips Disappear

Unofficial re-uploads of NBA broadcast footage violate the league's copyright, and they're routinely taken down. That's why a clip you find shared on a forum or social app one day can vanish the next — official league and network channels are the only reliably permanent source.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I watch official NBA highlights?

The NBA App, NBA.com, the league's YouTube channel, and ESPN/Bleacher Report for nationally relevant games.

How soon after a game do highlights get posted?

Top plays often post within minutes on social media; full condensed recaps are usually up within one to two hours.

Why do some NBA highlight videos get taken down?

Unofficial re-uploads violate NBA broadcast copyright and get removed via takedown requests.
