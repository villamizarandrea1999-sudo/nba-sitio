Dylan Harper Injury Update: Spurs Guard Ruled Out After Slipping vs. Pistons

Harper slipped late in the loss that ended San Antonio's 12-game winning streak. Here's his current status, what the Spurs have said, the reported timelines and the games ahead, updated with every new report.

By Davis Vance • Published November 18, 2026 • 4 min read
LATEST UPDATE · NOV. 18, 2026

Dylan Harper is out for Thursday's game against the Clippers with what the Spurs list as left hip soreness. He slipped with 53.6 seconds left in Tuesday's 105-93 loss to Detroit and was helped off the floor. There is no official diagnosis or return date yet, and further testing was expected Wednesday.

What Happened to Dylan Harper

The game was all but over. With 53.6 seconds left and Detroit in control, Harper ran up the floor as Stephon Castle brought the ball up behind him. According to ESPN, Harper appeared to slip on a wet spot just as Castle threw a pass his way, and Ausar Thompson picked up the loose ball.

Harper's left foot went out from under him and he grabbed his left groin area right away. Keldon Johnson and David Jones Garcia helped him to the tunnel.

"It just looked like he slipped obviously," coach Mitch Johnson said after the game. "So, hoping for the best. But don't know anything yet." Victor Wembanyama called it "a terrible feeling, terrible sight."

How Long Will Harper Be Out?

Nobody outside the Spurs knows yet. The team's only official word is the hip soreness designation and the Thursday ruling. A diagnosis should come after the tests.

The estimates going around are just that. Spurs reporter Dusty Garza said a groin injury like this can cost anywhere from a few days to six weeks, depending on what the MRI shows. Others have floated a two-to-six-week range if it's a moderate adductor strain. None of those come from the team.

The Spurs' schedule around Harper's injury. Thursday vs. the Clippers is the only game he has officially been ruled out of. Chart by NBA GRID.

What It Means for the Spurs

Harper has been one of San Antonio's main scorers next to Wembanyama. He entered Tuesday averaging 18.3 points, 5.5 rebounds and 4.7 assists in 14 games. He scored 20 on 8-of-14 shooting against Detroit, two nights after a team-high 31 at Utah.

Even before the slip, it was a rough night. Detroit ended the Spurs' 12-game winning streak, and Wembanyama's 25 points and 18 rebounds weren't enough. San Antonio is 12-3.

If Harper misses time, De'Aaron Fox and Castle take on more of the ball-handling, and HEAVY expects Julian Champagnie or Harrison Barnes to move into the starting lineup. For where the Spurs stood before the injury, see our NBA power rankings.

The Spurs list it as left hip soreness. There is no official diagnosis yet. Early reports described a groin injury, and some analysts suspect an adductor (groin) strain, but the team has not confirmed that.
