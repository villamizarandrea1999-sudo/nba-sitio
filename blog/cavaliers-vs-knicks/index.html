The Nov. 25 game counts twice: it's part of the regular-season standings and the Emirates NBA Cup group stage. Last season's Cup run ended with the Knicks lifting the trophy in Las Vegas, so expect New York to treat it like a big one.

Cavaliers vs Knicks Result: Cavs Win 115-107 at MSG (Nov. 11)

James Harden picked a good night to wake up. The 37-year-old came in averaging 15.3 points and shooting 36.4% from the field, then dropped a season-high 32 on 9-of-14 shooting at Madison Square Garden. He went 4-for-4 in the second quarter alone, with three 3-pointers, for 13 points as Cleveland built a 59-53 halftime lead.

The Knicks kept coming. Down 95-85 with 8:25 left, they ripped off a 14-4 run to tie it at 101. That's when Harden hit the go-ahead 3 with 4:21 to play. Evan Mobley scored, then Jarrett Allen took over the paint with four straight points, capped by a dunk that made it 110-103. Allen finished with 11 points and 14 rebounds.

Jalen Brunson scored 27 on 12-of-23 shooting but didn't attempt a single free throw. Karl-Anthony Towns had 21 points, 11 rebounds and 8 assists, his 11th straight double-double to open the season, which ties Willis Reed's franchise record. The loss snapped New York's four-game winning streak and ended Cleveland's two-game skid. Records after the game: Cavaliers 6-6, Knicks 6-5.

Key Matchups to Watch

Brunson vs. Mitchell and Harden

This rivalry runs through the backcourts. Brunson has scored 27 and 31 in the two games, and he torched Harden in the playoffs: in Game 1 of the East finals he kept attacking him to spark the 18-1 run that erased Cleveland's lead. Mitchell and Harden have split the big nights, 37 for Mitchell in the first game and 32 for Harden in the second. In Cleveland's one win, both scored 20-plus.

Towns vs. Allen and Mobley

Towns has been a problem for Cleveland's twin towers, with 29 and 13 in the first meeting and a near triple-double in the second. Allen missed the Nov. 7 game, and the Cavs' frontcourt looked completely different with him back on Nov. 11: 14 rebounds and the game-sealing dunk. Towns' double-double streak (11 straight to start the season, tied with Willis Reed's Knicks record) goes on the line next at Indiana on Nov. 13.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the next Cavaliers vs Knicks game?

The next Cavaliers vs Knicks game is Wednesday, November 25, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. ET at Madison Square Garden in New York. It is an Emirates NBA Cup group-stage game (East Group B). After that, the teams meet on Monday, December 21, at 7:00 p.m. ET in Cleveland.

What channel is Cavaliers vs Knicks on?

The Cleveland Cavaliers won the last game, 115-107 at Madison Square Garden on November 11, 2026. James Harden scored a season-high 32 points, including the go-ahead 3-pointer with 4:21 left.

What is the Cavaliers vs Knicks season series?

The 2026-27 season series is tied 1-1. The Knicks won 109-97 in Cleveland on November 7, and the Cavaliers won 115-107 in New York on November 11. Two games remain: November 25 and December 21.

Yes. The Knicks swept the Cavaliers 4-0 in the 2026 Eastern Conference finals, starting with a comeback from 22 points down in the fourth quarter of Game 1, then beat the Spurs in the NBA Finals.
