2026-27 NBA Power Rankings: Preseason Top 10

A wild offseason of trades and free-agent moves reshuffled the league's pecking order. Here's how the top of the NBA looks before tip-off on October 20.

By NBA GRID Staff • September 10, 2026 • 5 min read

Between LeBron James joining Philadelphia, Giannis Antetokounmpo's move to Miami, and a six-team blockbuster reshaping the West, the 2026-27 NBA power rankings look nothing like last spring's Finals bracket. Here's our preseason top 10.

Offseason movement reshuffled contender status across both conferences.

KEY TAKEAWAY

The defending-champion Knicks, the Finals-runner-up Spurs and a retooled 76ers roster headline the preseason top tier, with Miami and Cleveland pushing into the conversation after major offseason additions.
