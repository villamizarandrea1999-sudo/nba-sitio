Luka Doncic Injury Update: Is He Playing? Latest Status and Injury History

Luka Doncic is healthy and playing. Here's his current status, what happened with his ankle and back this season, the hamstring injury that cost him the 2026 playoffs, and his full injury history, updated whenever his status changes.

By Davis Vance • Published November 21, 2026 • 6 min read
LATEST UPDATE · NOV. 21, 2026

Luka Doncic is not injured right now. He is off the Lakers' injury report and has played five straight games since spraining his right ankle at Milwaukee on Nov. 11, including 43 points at Phoenix four days later. He has missed two games this season, both with low back tightness. The Lakers' next game is Monday, Nov. 23, vs. Utah.

Is Luka Doncic Playing Tonight?

Unless something changes, yes. Doncic was not on the Lakers' latest injury report, and he played 36 minutes in Friday's 106-104 win over Sacramento. The Lakers are 14-3 and next play Utah at home on Monday, Nov. 23. If he shows up on the report as questionable or out, we'll update the status box above.

The Right Ankle Sprain at Milwaukee (Nov. 11)

With the Lakers up big and just over four minutes left at Milwaukee, Doncic hit a step-back jumper over Kyle Kuzma and landed on Kuzma's foot. His right ankle rolled. He was fouled on the play, stayed in to take the free throw, then went to the locker room. He finished with 32 points, five rebounds and five assists in 32 minutes of a 123-112 win.

"I'm good, obviously it's painful a little bit but I'll be fine," Doncic said after the game. JJ Redick called it "just a light tweak." The next day, ESPN's Shams Charania reported that Doncic woke up without pain in the ankle. He wasn't on the injury report for the Phoenix game, and he scored 43 in a 131-123 win.

Back Tightness: The Two Games He Has Missed

Doncic's only absences this season have come from his back. He sat out Oct. 28 at the Clippers and Nov. 8 at Detroit with low back tightness, and both times he was back in the lineup the next game. The Lakers won both games without him. He has played 15 of the team's 17 games.

The Hamstring Injury That Cost Him the 2026 Playoffs

The injury that still shapes how people talk about Doncic's health came last season. On April 2, 2026, at Oklahoma City, he strained his left hamstring in the first half, was cleared to return, and lasted about four more minutes. The diagnosis was a Grade 2 strain of the left hamstring, per the AP. It was his second left hamstring strain of the season; he had already missed four games before the All-Star break.

He missed the Lakers' final 15 games, including all 10 in the playoffs. Reporting from Athlon said further evaluation in Spain found a deeper injury than the first MRI showed, and that he had platelet-rich plasma and stem cell treatment there. The Lakers upset Houston in the first round without him, then were swept by Oklahoma City. Doncic said in May that he had not been close to returning.
