Donovan Mitchell Signs $273M Max Extension with Cavaliers

Fresh off his first career trip to the conference finals, Donovan Mitchell has committed his long-term future to Cleveland.

By Staff Reporter • July 7, 2026 • 4 min read

Donovan Mitchell has agreed to a four-year, $273 million maximum contract extension with the Cleveland Cavaliers, locking in the centerpiece of the franchise's championship window for years to come.

Mitchell's new deal keeps him in Cleveland through the rest of the decade.

QUICK TAKEAWAY

The extension includes a player option for the 2030-31 season and a full no-trade kicker — a strong signal of mutual long-term commitment.

The Deal

The new extension carries a player option for the 2030-31 season and a full trade kicker, giving Mitchell both long-term security and leverage. It's the second extension he's signed since arriving in Cleveland via trade in 2022, following a three-year, $150.3 million maximum extension in 2024. Notably, Mitchell signed on the very first day he became extension-eligible rather than waiting — a move that actually cost him money. Per ESPN, had he waited until next summer, he could have signed a five-year, $353 million deal, worth roughly $80 million more than what he agreed to now.

Why Now

The timing follows a breakthrough postseason for Mitchell and the Cavaliers, who reached the Eastern Conference Finals for the first time in his career this past spring before being eliminated by the New York Knicks. Locking in Mitchell now — rather than letting extension talks linger into free agency — gives Cleveland's front office cost certainty and reinforces the roster's stability heading into 2026-27.

What It Means for Cleveland's Future

Beyond simply retaining a star, the structure of the deal is being read around the league as a move that preserves financial flexibility for the Cavaliers — flexibility some reports connect to Cleveland positioning itself for future star additions. Whatever comes next, the extension makes clear that Mitchell is the player the Cavaliers are building around.
