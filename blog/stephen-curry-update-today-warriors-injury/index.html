Stephen Curry Update Today: Warriors Reveal Oblique Tear, Re-Evaluation Timeline

Curry tore an abdominal oblique at Houston and will be re-evaluated in two weeks. Here's his current status, what the Warriors and reporters have said about a timeline, and the games he's set to miss, updated with every new report.

By Davis Vance • Published November 16, 2026 • 5 min read
LATEST UPDATE · NOV. 16, 2026, BEFORE WARRIORS AT PELICANS

Stephen Curry is out with an oblique abdominal tear he suffered in the first quarter at Houston on Nov. 11. An MRI confirmed the tear, and the Warriors say he will be re-evaluated in two weeks, around Nov. 26. There is no official return date. ESPN's Shams Charania reports Curry is expected to miss closer to a month.

What Happened to Stephen Curry

Curry hit a 3-pointer early in the first quarter at Houston on Nov. 11, then grabbed at the right side of his abdomen. He went to the locker room at the Warriors' first timeout, with 6:25 left in the quarter, and never came back. He finished with six points in six minutes, and Golden State lost 115-108. That loss also ended the Warriors' eight-game winning streak.

The MRI came that night. On Nov. 12 the team announced the result: an oblique abdominal tear, with a re-evaluation in two weeks. Steve Kerr had said before the scan that his concern was "high." Afterward, he told 95.7 The Game it was "as good as we could've expected."

How Long Will Curry Be Out?

Read the official wording carefully. Two weeks is when the Warriors will re-evaluate Curry, not when he is due back. Kerr made that clear after practice on Nov. 15: "It means we have no idea right now. We'll tell you in two weeks."

The longer estimate comes from reporting, not the team. Charania said on ESPN that Curry is expected to be sidelined "closer to one month," and that the Warriors are bracing for him to miss a double-digit number of games.

The schedule puts those numbers in context. Curry will miss at least six games before the re-evaluation. If he's out about a month, the count is nine through Dec. 1, because Golden State has no games from Dec. 2 to Dec. 11. Double digits would mean he's still out at Cleveland on Dec. 12.

The Warriors' schedule around Curry's injury. The re-evaluation is the only official date. Chart by NBA GRID.

What It Means for the Warriors

The first full game without Curry was ugly. San Antonio won 123-85 in an NBA Cup game on Nov. 13, with Stephon Castle scoring 32. LJ Cryer led Golden State with 20. Yaxel Lendeborg, who scored 25 with 10 rebounds the night Curry got hurt, went 1-of-13 for three points. The Warriors fell to 8-5.

Golden State is also without Jimmy Butler (ACL), Moses Moody, Kristaps Porzingis and Brandon Williams (hamstring), so there's little depth to lean on. Expect Lendeborg to keep the biggest scoring load. For more on where the team stood before the injury, see our NBA power rankings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What injury does Stephen Curry have?

Stephen Curry has an oblique abdominal tear, confirmed by an MRI on the night of November 11, 2026. The NBA injury report lists it as a right oblique tear.

When did Stephen Curry get hurt?

Curry got hurt early in the first quarter of the Warriors' 115-108 loss at Houston on November 11, 2026. He left the game with 6:25 remaining in the first quarter and did not return. He played six minutes and scored six points.

How long will Stephen Curry be out?

Officially, the Warriors only said Curry will be re-evaluated in two weeks. ESPN's Shams Charania reported he is expected to be out closer to one month, and that the team is bracing for him to miss a double-digit number of games. Neither is an official return date.

When will Stephen Curry be re-evaluated?

The two-week re-evaluation points to around November 26, 2026 (Thanksgiving). Steve Kerr said the team will not give interim updates before then.

Is there an official return date for Stephen Curry?

No. The Warriors have not announced a return date. The re-evaluation in two weeks is a checkpoint, not a return.

How many games will Stephen Curry miss?

By the schedule, at least six: the Nov. 13 game at San Antonio plus Nov. 16 at New Orleans, Nov. 18 at Dallas, Nov. 19 at Memphis, Nov. 22 vs. Boston and Nov. 25 vs. Sacramento, all before the re-evaluation. If he misses close to a month, that becomes nine games through Dec. 1, because the Warriors have no games from Dec. 2 to Dec. 11.

How have the Warriors done without Stephen Curry?

In their first full game without him, the Warriors lost 123-85 at San Antonio in an NBA Cup game on November 13, falling to 8-5. Their eight-game winning streak had already ended in the Houston loss, the night Curry got hurt.
