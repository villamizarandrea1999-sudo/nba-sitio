NBA Games: Schedule, Live Scores & How to Watch

A quick reference for how the NBA schedule works, where to check live scores, and how to catch every game — regular season through the Finals.

By Staff Reporter • 5 min read

"NBA games" run nearly every night for more than half the year — from the October tip-off through the June Finals. Looking for today's matchups and tip-off times? Here's the fastest way to find them, plus how the schedule works and how to watch.

Today's & This Week's NBA Games Schedule

FIND TONIGHT'S GAMES

Regular-season games run from October through April, followed by the play-in and playoffs.

How Many NBA Games Are There in a Season?

Every team plays 82 regular-season games — 41 at home, 41 on the road — for 1,230 total games league-wide. That's followed by the play-in tournament (seeds 7 through 10 in each conference) and a four-round playoff bracket that ends with the NBA Finals in June.

Where to Check Live Scores

For real-time scores, box scores, and play-by-play, the official sources are NBA.com and ESPN's NBA scoreboard — both update live during every game. We don't run a live scoreboard here, so we'd rather point you to the source than show you stale numbers.
