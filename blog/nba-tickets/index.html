NBA Tickets: How & Where to Buy Cheap Seats

Whether you're chasing courtside seats or the cheapest ticket in the building, here's how to buy NBA tickets safely — and how to avoid overpaying.

By Staff Reporter • 6 min read

Buying NBA tickets comes down to two decisions: which marketplace to trust, and when to pull the trigger. Get both right and you can land tickets well under face value. Get them wrong and you risk paying a premium — or worse, buying a ticket that never scans at the door.

A full house on a rivalry night — the exact kind of game where ticket prices spike fastest.

QUICK TAKEAWAY

The biggest price drops usually happen in the final 24-48 hours before tipoff for non-marquee matchups — patience pays off.

When to Buy for the Cheapest Price

  • Last 24-48 hours before tipoff — sellers drop prices to avoid unsold inventory, especially for non-marquee matchups.
  • Weeknight, non-rivalry games — consistently cheaper than weekend or nationally televised rivalry games.
  • Upper-level corners — often the biggest single jump in value per dollar compared to sideline seats one section over.
  • Avoid opening week and playoffs — demand (and price) peaks hardest here.

Red Flags That Mean You're About to Get Scammed

  • Seller asks for payment via bank transfer, cash app, or gift cards outside the platform's checkout.
  • You're sent a PDF or screenshot instead of a transfer through the official team app.
  • The listing is priced dramatically below every comparable seat for a high-demand game.
  • The marketplace has no buyer guarantee or refund policy for invalid tickets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the safest place to buy NBA tickets?

The team's official box office, Ticketmaster, or a verified resale marketplace with a buyer guarantee like StubHub, SeatGeek, or Vivid Seats.

What is the cheapest day to buy NBA tickets?

Usually the final 24-48 hours before tipoff for lower-demand matchups, as sellers try to avoid unsold inventory.

How can I avoid getting scammed buying NBA tickets?

Stick to platforms with a buyer guarantee, avoid off-platform payment requests, and confirm transfer through the official team app before the event.
