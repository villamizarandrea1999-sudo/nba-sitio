Immaculate Grid Basketball: Play Today's NBA 3x3 Grid

Play our free daily NBA 3x3 grid right here: name a player for every team, award and stat square. Below it: how the official Immaculate Grid basketball game works, where to play it, and how to go immaculate.

By Davis Vance • Published July 25, 2026 • Updated November 11, 2026 • 6 min read

NBA Immaculate Grid is a daily basketball trivia puzzle. It's a 3x3 grid where every row and every column stands for a category, like a team, a stat milestone, a draft class or an award. For each of the nine squares, you have to name one player who fits both the row and the column, and you can't use the same player twice anywhere on the board.

    How to Play Our NBA 3x3 Grid

    Our grid at the top of this page plays like the Immaculate Grid basketball game, with a few differences. Every day you get a new 3x3 board. The rows and columns are NBA franchises, awards, single-season stat marks, decades or "teammate of" a star. Click a square, start typing a name, and pick the player from the list.

    • Three strikes, not nine guesses. A wrong answer costs a strike. Three strikes and the grid is over, so you can keep guessing on a square until you run out.
    • Deep Cut score. Every correct square shows how deep a cut your pick was, from 0% (the player with the most career games among all valid answers) to 100% (the one with the fewest). Your score is the average across all nine squares.
    • Franchise history counts. A SuperSonics player counts for the Thunder, a Bobcats player counts for the Hornets, and a Washington Bullets player counts for the Wizards.
    • NBA seasons only. Stats, awards and teams come from NBA (and BAA) seasons on Basketball-Reference. ABA seasons don't count. Season marks need at least 40 games played.
    • "Teammate of" means the two players appeared for the same team in the same season.
    • Archive. Missed a day? Pick any past grid from the menu above the board. Archive grids don't count toward your streak.

    When the grid ends, tap any square to see every player who fits it. The player data was last updated from Basketball-Reference on November 11, 2026, and we refresh it during the season.

    How Scoring Works in the Official Immaculate Grid

    • Immaculate: every square correct on the first try, with zero wrong guesses.
    • Correct, not immaculate: you filled in all nine squares but burned at least one wrong guess along the way.
    • Rarity score: most versions also grade how obscure your correct answers were compared with other players, so deep-cut picks beat the obvious superstar answer.

    Strategy for Going Immaculate

    1. Start with your most certain square: lock in the answers you know are right first, so you can see exactly what's left to solve.
    2. Think journeymen, not superstars: a player who bounced around six teams is often a better fit for an odd team/team pairing than an all-time great who spent his whole career on one roster.
    3. Save your riskiest square for last: if you're going to guess wrong, it's better to do it on the square that gives you the least new information anyway.
    4. Watch for players who fit an obvious cell almost too well: the crowd has usually picked them already, and that tanks your rarity score even when you get the square right.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Where can I play Immaculate Grid basketball?

    The official Immaculate Grid basketball game is free on Sports Reference. You can also play our own daily NBA 3x3 grid at the top of this page, with a new board every day and an archive of past grids.

    Is there a new NBA grid every day?

    Yes. Both the official Immaculate Grid and our NBA 3x3 grid post a new puzzle every day. Ours switches over at midnight your local time.

    How does the Deep Cut score work?

    In our grid, each correct answer is scored from 0% to 100% based on how many career games the player has compared with every other valid answer for that square. Fewer games means a deeper cut and a higher score.

    What is NBA Immaculate Grid?

    A daily puzzle where you fill a 3x3 grid, naming a player who satisfies each row-and-column category pair without repeating any player.

    What does "immaculate" mean in NBA Immaculate Grid?

    Filling all nine squares correctly on the first guess each time, with zero wrong answers.

    What's a good strategy for NBA Immaculate Grid?

    Lock in your most certain squares first, lean on well-traveled journeyman players for odd category pairs, and save your riskiest guess for last.
