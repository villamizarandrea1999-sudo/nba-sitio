Every 6'7" NBA Player in 2026-27

6'7" is the most common height in the NBA this season. Here are all 61 players listed at 6'7", the best of them now and ever, and how LaMelo Ball turned the number into a meme.

By Davis Vance • Published November 5, 2026 • 8 min read
QUICK ANSWER

61 players on 2026-27 NBA rosters are listed at 6'7", more than at any other height. The best known include LaMelo Ball, OG Anunoby, Khris Middleton and the Thompson twins. The greatest 6'7" player ever is Julius Erving.

6'7" is the peak of the league's height curve: 61 of 526 rostered players. Chart by NBA GRID from team rosters on Basketball-Reference.

6'7" Is the NBA's Most Common Height

We went through all 30 team rosters for the 2026-27 season (526 players, two-way contracts included) and counted every listed height. No height shows up more often than 6'7": 61 players, about 12% of the league. The next most crowded spots are 6'5" (57 players) and 6'6" (56).

6'7" is also the league's median height. Line every rostered player up from shortest to tallest and the middle of the line is 6'7": 246 players are shorter and 219 are taller. The average works out to about 6'6.8". So if you are wondering whether 6'7" is tall for an NBA player, the honest answer is that it is the most typical size in the league. Most 6'7" players are wings and forwards, but a few run the backcourt. Listed as point guards: Josh Giddey (Chicago Bulls), LaMelo Ball (Minnesota Timberwolves) and Anthony Black (Orlando Magic). Listed as shooting guards: Dailyn Swain (Chicago Bulls), Gradey Dick (LA Clippers), Jaylen Wells (Memphis Grizzlies), Caris LeVert (Milwaukee Bucks) and Micah Peavy (Oklahoma City Thunder). A 6'7" point guard can see over most defenses, which helps explain why Ball and Josh Giddey pass so well.

Why 6'7" Is a Meme Now: LaMelo Ball and "6-7"

If you have heard kids yelling "six-seven," this is why. The phrase comes from "Doot Doot (6 7)", a song by Philadelphia rapper Skrilla, first released in December 2024 and officially released on February 7, 2025. TikTok users began setting the song's beat drop to highlight clips of LaMelo Ball, who is listed at 6'7", and the edits took off in 2025.

From there it became an all-purpose joke on TikTok and Instagram with no fixed meaning, and Skrilla himself has said the meaning is open. In October 2025, Dictionary.com named "67" its Word of the Year. For basketball fans the link is simple: 6'7" is LaMelo's height, and it is the most common height in the league.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which NBA players are 6'7"?

61 players on 2026-27 NBA rosters are listed at 6'7", including LaMelo Ball, OG Anunoby, Khris Middleton, Amen and Ausar Thompson, Dyson Daniels and Brandon Miller. 12 of the 61 are on two-way contracts.

Is 6'7" tall for an NBA player?

No. 6'7" is almost exactly average. It is the league's median height and its most common one this season: of 526 rostered players, 246 are shorter and 219 are taller. The average listed height is about 6'6.8".

Who is the best 6'7" NBA player of all time?

Julius Erving has the strongest case. Dr. J was an 11-time NBA All-Star, the 1981 NBA MVP, a three-time ABA MVP and won three titles (two ABA, one NBA). Rick Barry, Clyde Drexler and Paul Pierce are also Hall of Famers listed at 6'7".

Are Kawhi Leonard and Jimmy Butler 6'7"?

Not on their current listings. Both have often been described as 6'7", but their 2026-27 roster listings show 6'6" (Leonard with Toronto, Butler with Golden State), so they are not in our list.

What does "6-7" mean, and what does LaMelo Ball have to do with it?

"6-7" comes from the song "Doot Doot (6 7)" by Philadelphia rapper Skrilla. TikTok edits paired the song with highlights of LaMelo Ball, who is listed at 6'7", and the phrase spread from there. Dictionary.com named "67" its 2025 Word of the Year.

What NBA player is 7'6"?

Tacko Fall, who is on a two-way contract with the Philadelphia 76ers, is listed at 7'6" on 2026-27 rosters, the tallest listing of any player attached to an NBA team this season.
