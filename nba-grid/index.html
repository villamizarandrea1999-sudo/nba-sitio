Pick a square and name an NBA player who fits both its row and its column. Each player can be used once. You get three strikes. Rarer correct answers earn a higher Deep Cut score. A new grid goes up every day at midnight your time.

    How to Play NBA GRID

    Every row and column of the 3x3 board is a category: an NBA franchise, an award, a single-season stat mark, a decade, or "teammate of" a star. Tap a square, start typing a player's name and pick him from the list. He has to fit both the row and the column, and you can only use each player once.

    • Three strikes: a wrong answer costs a strike. After three, the board is over.
    • Deep Cut score: rarer correct answers score higher, based on career games compared with every other valid answer for that square.
    • Franchise history counts: a SuperSonics player counts for the Thunder, a Bobcats player for the Hornets.
    • A new board at midnight your local time, plus an archive of every past grid in the menu above the board.
