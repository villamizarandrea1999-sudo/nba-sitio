Davis Vance is a sports journalist who covers the NBA for NBA GRID. He has a degree in Journalism and Communication and a master's degree in Sports Journalism, and basketball, especially the NBA, is the sport he's most passionate about.

What Davis Covers

  • Injury updates: what happened, what the team has officially said, and realistic return timelines.
  • Trades, contracts and league news: the moves that reshape rosters, explained in a few minutes of reading.
  • Season guides: schedules, standings, the preseason and everything fans need to follow the season.
  • Fan explainers: straight answers to the questions people actually search for, from how to watch games to buying tickets.

How He Reports

  • Official sources first. Team medical updates, league announcements and official schedules come before anything else.
  • Reporting is attributed. When information comes from another outlet or a reporter, the article says who reported it and links to it.
  • Health details come from medical authorities. When an injury needs context, it's drawn from sources such as the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons and MedlinePlus, not guesswork.
  • Dates are transparent. Every article shows when it was published and, when the content changes, when it was updated.
