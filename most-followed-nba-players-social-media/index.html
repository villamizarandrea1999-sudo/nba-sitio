Most Followed NBA Players on Social Media in 2026

Who has the biggest following on Instagram, X and TikTok, from LeBron James to the young stars catching up. Counted from official profiles and updated monthly.

By Davis Vance • Published October 31, 2026 • 8 min read

Combined followers on Instagram, X and TikTok, active players, October 31, 2026.

LeBron James is the most followed NBA player on social media, with 208.9 million followers across Instagram, X and TikTok combined, and he is also the most followed NBA player on Instagram with 153.6 million. Below you'll find the full top 10 across all platforms, a separate Instagram ranking, a LeBron James breakdown, profiles of every player, and the young players worth following.

Last updated: October 31, 2026. Every number was read from the player's official profile on that date and rounded to one decimal in millions. "Total" adds Instagram, X and TikTok; "—" means we found no verified official account. The ranking covers active players only.

Player profiles: the top 10

1. LeBron James, Philadelphia 76ers

Accounts: Instagram @kingjames (153.6M), X @KingJames (55.3M). Total: 208.9M, or 45% of all followers in this top 10.

The NBA's all-time leading scorer is in his 24th season, his first with Philadelphia, after four championships and four MVP awards.

2. Stephen Curry, Golden State Warriors

Accounts: Instagram @stephencurry30 (56.9M), X @StephenCurry30 (24.6M), TikTok @stephencurry30 (9.1M). Total: 90.6M, or 20% of all followers in this top 10.

The NBA's all-time leader in made three-pointers has four championships and two MVPs, including the league's only unanimous MVP in 2016.

3. Kevin Durant, Houston Rockets

Accounts: Instagram @easymoneysniper (13.4M), X @KDTrey5 (26.8M). Total: 40.2M, or 9% of all followers in this top 10.

A two-time champion and two-time Finals MVP, Durant won the 2014 MVP and is the only player with four Olympic gold medals in men's basketball.

4. Giannis Antetokounmpo, Miami Heat

Accounts: Instagram @giannis_an34 (16.1M), X @Giannis_An34 (3.4M), TikTok @giannis_an34 (6.3M). Total: 25.8M, or 6% of all followers in this top 10.

The two-time MVP and 2021 Finals MVP was traded from Milwaukee to Miami this offseason, ending 13 seasons with the Bucks.

5. Kyrie Irving, Dallas Mavericks

Accounts: Instagram @kyrieirving (19.8M), X @KyrieIrving (4.4M), TikTok @kyrieirving (1.4M). Total: 25.6M, or 6% of all followers in this top 10.

Irving hit the go-ahead three in Game 7 of the 2016 Finals, delivering Cleveland's only championship alongside LeBron James.

6. James Harden, Cleveland Cavaliers

Accounts: Instagram @jharden13 (11.8M), X @JHarden13 (7.3M), TikTok @jharden13 (0.6M). Total: 19.7M, or 4% of all followers in this top 10.

The 2018 MVP has won three scoring titles and sits among the top players all time in made three-pointers.

7. Luka Dončić, Los Angeles Lakers

Accounts: Instagram @lukadoncic (10.8M), TikTok @lukadoncic (2.8M). Total: 13.6M, or 3% of all followers in this top 10.

The six-time All-Star led the NBA in scoring last season and is now the face of the Lakers.

8. Bronny James, Los Angeles Lakers

Accounts: Instagram @bronny (7.4M), TikTok @bronny (6.0M). Total: 13.4M, or 3% of all followers in this top 10.

In October 2024, Bronny and LeBron became the first father and son to play together in an NBA game.

9. Ja Morant, Portland Trail Blazers

Accounts: Instagram @jamorant (9.3M), X @JaMorant (3.0M). Total: 12.3M, or 3% of all followers in this top 10.

Morant was the 2020 Rookie of the Year and the 2022 Most Improved Player.

10. LaMelo Ball, Minnesota Timberwolves

Accounts: Instagram @melo (10.7M), X @MELOD1P (1.0M), TikTok @melo (0.4M). Total: 12.1M, or 3% of all followers in this top 10.

The 2021 Rookie of the Year built an online following in high school and now plays for Minnesota.

FAQ

Who is the most followed NBA player?

LeBron James is the most followed NBA player, with 208.9 million followers across Instagram, X and TikTok as of October 31, 2026.

Who is the most followed active NBA player on Instagram?

LeBron James has the most Instagram followers of any active NBA player, with 153.6 million. Stephen Curry is second with 56.9 million.

How we update this list

Every follower count on this page is read directly from each player's official Instagram, X and TikTok profile, never copied from other articles, and the date pulled is shown at the top. Instagram counts are exact; X and TikTok display follower counts rounded to one decimal, which is the precision we use. Facebook is not included because public pages only show large follower counts rounded to the nearest million, too coarse for a ranking like this. We refresh the numbers monthly; if you spot a change in team or a new official account, email davis.vance@nba-grid.com.
