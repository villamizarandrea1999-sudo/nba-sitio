How the 2026-27 NBA Standings Work

Each team plays 82 regular-season games, from opening night on October 20, 2026, to the final day on April 11, 2027. The standings are split by conference, and that's what decides the playoff field: 15 teams in the East, 15 in the West.

  • Seeds 1–6 clinch a playoff spot outright.
  • Seeds 7–10 play in the play-in tournament for the final two spots in each conference.
  • Seeds 11–15 are eliminated and head to the draft lottery.
  • NBA Cup games count, including the group stage, quarterfinals and semifinals. Only the Cup championship game is left out.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many teams make the NBA playoffs?

Sixteen. The top six teams in each conference qualify directly. Teams seeded 7 through 10 go to the play-in tournament, which decides the last two playoff spots in each conference.

How does the NBA play-in tournament work?

The No. 7 seed hosts No. 8, and the winner earns the 7th seed. No. 9 hosts No. 10, and the loser is eliminated. The loser of the 7-8 game then hosts the winner of the 9-10 game for the 8th seed.

Do NBA Cup games count in the standings?

Yes, except one. NBA Cup group-stage games, quarterfinals and semifinals all count in the regular-season standings. The Cup championship game does not.

When does the 2026-27 NBA regular season end?

The regular season ends on April 11, 2027, after each team plays 82 games.

How are ties broken in the NBA standings?

The NBA uses tiebreakers such as head-to-head record, division record and conference record. Our table orders tied teams by winning percentage and then total wins, so the official seed can differ when two teams have the same record.
