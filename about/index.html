About NBA GRID

An independent NBA site built around one idea: give fans fast news, real puzzles, and zero filler.

NBA GRID covers the NBA the way we'd want to read it ourselves — live scores and schedules, weekly news roundups on trades and contracts, and a set of original daily puzzle games (our Connections-style Daily Grid, with more grid formats in the works) for fans who want more than just headlines.

What We Do

  • NBA Today & NBA Games — schedules, live scores, and player birthdays in one place.
  • Weekly news coverage — trades, contract extensions, and league storylines, written to be read in a few minutes.
  • Daily puzzle games — original NBA-themed grid puzzles for fans who like trivia with their basketball.

Who's Behind It

NBA GRID is run by a small, independent editorial team — no league affiliation, no team bias. We're fans first, and we built this site to be the kind of NBA resource we wanted to use ourselves: quick to load, easy to scan, and free of clickbait.

Corrections & Contact

Spot an error or have a tip? Reach us at hello@nba-grid.com. We correct mistakes as soon as we can verify them.
