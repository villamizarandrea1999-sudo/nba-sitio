About NBA GRID

A quick map of everything you'll find on NBA GRID — news, live scores, daily puzzles, and explainer guides.

NBA GRID is an independent NBA site covering the league across four types of content: breaking news, live scores & schedules, original daily puzzle games, and explainer guides that answer specific fan questions in plain language.

News & Trades

Coverage of trades, contract extensions, league discipline, and front-office moves as they happen — written to be read in a few minutes, not fifteen. See the latest news feed on the homepage or browse the full archive.

Live Scores & Schedule

NBA Games covers the schedule and live scores. NBA Today is the daily hub for what's happening across the league, including player birthdays and the day's slate.

Daily Puzzle Games

Our Daily Grid is an original, Connections-style puzzle where you sort 16 NBA names into four hidden categories. We also cover how to play similar games like NBA Immaculate Grid and NBA Connections.

Explainer Guides

Straight answers to specific questions fans actually search for — things like who's the tallest player in the league right now, the shortest, how many players are in the NBA, and how to watch games live.

Corrections & Contact

Spot an error or have a tip? Reach us at hello@nba-grid.com. We correct mistakes as soon as we can verify them.
